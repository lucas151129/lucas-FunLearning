Forge notes

English builds new words by bolting pieces onto a core. Read the clue, then pick pieces that fit both the meaning and the order.

  • Prefix — before the root. un- = not, pre- = before.
  • Root — the core meaning. port = carry, view = see.
  • Suffix — after the root; often changes the word class. -able = can be, -less = without.
